Vibe coders are getting sued.

The app you hacked together and shipped over the weekend is already breaking 6 laws without you knowing:

1. Signup doesn't ask for age. Let in one kid under 13 and that's a COPPA violation, up to $53K, counted per child.

2. Load Google Fonts from their servers and every visitor's IP gets sent over. A Munich court ordered one site to pay a visitor €100 for it under GDPR.

3. Use session replay to record what users type without consent, and in California you get sued for wiretapping under CIPA, up to $5K per violation.

4. Marketing emails with no unsubscribe link or postal address violate CAN-SPAM, up to $53K per email.

5. If renewal terms aren't right next to the subscribe button, California treats every renewal as an unconditional gift. You have to give the money back.

6. Skip the $6 DMCA agent registration with the Copyright Office and you're on the hook for whatever infringing content users upload. Willful infringement can cost up to $150K per work.

Each of these takes minutes to fix. Throw this at Claude Code before you launch:
"Check my app for: missing age gate, externally loaded fonts, session replay without consent, marketing emails without unsubscribe and address, renewal terms not beside the subscribe button and no DMCA agent. Fix what you can and list what I need to do myself."
